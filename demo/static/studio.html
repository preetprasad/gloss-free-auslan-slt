    How it works
    1. Make room. Enable the camera and keep your upper body and both hands in the frame.
    2. Sign one thought. Choose a model, select Start signing, then Finish & translate when you’re done. Recording ends at 10 seconds. On a laptop, the translation arrives about 4–5 seconds after Finish for every second signed; the six-model ensemble adds about a second for image features.
    3. Review the result. Read the English text, play it aloud or copy it. If asked, try signing again.

    More than one person in view? The translator follows each person by where their shoulders are, calls them Person A, Person B and so on, and translates the one moving most like a signer. That choice is a guess, so the result says who was translated and, when someone else was in view for most of the utterance, offers a switch. Nobody is recognised by their face or appearance.

    Captured frames are sent to the server during recording and processing. Images are not saved. Capture buffers are released after completion, cancellation or disconnection; an in-flight computation may finish first. Turning the camera off stops its live feed. Use Clear to discard the current utterance.
