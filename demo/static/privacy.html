01Who runs this site

Auslan / English is a research showcase maintained by Preet Prasad for an Honours project at the University of Western Australia, supervised by Dr Atif Mansoor. This policy covers this project website; it is not a privacy policy for the University as a whole.

For privacy questions or requests, email @example.org.

Current public release

The public site shows recorded dataset examples. Live camera translation is unavailable on this deployment, so it does not capture or upload your camera video.

02When you browse

The walkthrough loads published video clips and saved model results from this site. It does not ask for camera or microphone access. There are no accounts, advertising trackers or application analytics in this release, and the application sets no cookies or persistent visitor identifiers.

Vercel hosts the website. Delivering and protecting the service involves technical request information, such as your IP address, browser or device information, requested URLs and request times. Hosting records are separate from the translation examples and are governed by Vercel’s Privacy Notice. This project does not set a separate retention period for those provider records. Vercel may process information in the United States and other countries.

Your browser may cache public page assets and clips. External links open services with their own privacy practices.

03When a translation server is connected

The software also supports a separate, server-backed research deployment. The following describes that mode, which is not currently available on the public Vercel site.

  • Camera: access starts only after you choose to enable it. The microphone is not requested. During a signed recording, video frames and capture times are sent to the connected translation server to extract movement coordinates and produce English text.
  • Processing: frames, coordinates and timing information are held in memory. Image-based models keep frames until image features have been computed. The application does not save recordings or add them to training data.
  • Cleanup: capture buffers are released after completion, cancellation or disconnection. A computation already running may finish before its buffers can be released. Temporary session identifiers, connection information and rate-limit counters support operation and abuse prevention.
  • Results: the current result and recent conversation history remain in the open browser page. They are not written to an application database or browser storage. Reloading or closing the page removes this application state. Copying text creates a separate copy on your clipboard.

Hosting and server infrastructure may keep technical or error logs. The application is designed to omit session content from its error messages; this is not a claim that all infrastructure logging is disabled.

04Your controls

In a connected translator, Finish or Cancel stops the current capture. Turning the camera off stops the live feed. Clear removes the current result; Clear conversation removes the visible history. You can also revoke camera permission in your browser.

Speech is optional. The application uses only voices reported by the browser as local to your device; it does not send text to a project-operated speech service. If no local English voice is available, speech remains unavailable. Device and browser services have their own privacy settings.

05Enquiries and requests

If you email the project, your address, message and any information you choose to include are processed through the University email service to respond to you. Email is separate from the website’s temporary translation session, and has no project-configured automatic deletion schedule. Please avoid sending signing videos or sensitive personal information unless we have first agreed how to handle them.

You can ask what information the project holds about you, request access, correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern by emailing the address above. We will explain what can be located and what action is possible; we cannot retrieve a translation session that was never saved. Provider-held records may require a request to that provider.

This policy will be revised when the public service’s data handling changes. The date at the top identifies this version.
