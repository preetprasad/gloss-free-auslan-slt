01About the service

Auslan / English is maintained by Preet Prasad as part of a University of Western Australia Honours research project. It explains research into translating Auslan video into English text and optional speech. It is a research prototype, not an accredited interpreting service or an official University service.

The public release provides recorded examples, research results and an experimental live translator that runs in your browser after a one-time model download that you choose to start (see the Privacy Policy). The translator checks, and shows, whether your browser and device can run it. Features may change or be unavailable.

02Understanding the output

Translations may be incomplete, incorrect or unrelated to the signing. Model confidence is not a probability that an answer is correct, and a high score does not establish that a person is signing. Recorded benchmark results do not establish accuracy for new webcam users.

Use the system for exploration and research. Do not rely on it for medical, legal, emergency or other consequential communication, or as a replacement for a qualified Auslan interpreter. Review English output before choosing to copy or speak it. See the research limitations for the evidence behind these boundaries.

03Using the site responsibly

Only submit a recording when you have permission from the people shown and the right to use it. Keep sensitive or confidential information out of experimental recordings. Do not use the service to impersonate someone, harass people, identify signers or misrepresent a machine-generated sentence as their verified words.

Do not bypass access restrictions, disrupt the service or use it to gain unauthorised access to data or systems. Responsible research and good-faith issue reports are welcome; send reports privately to @example.org without including another person’s private data.
